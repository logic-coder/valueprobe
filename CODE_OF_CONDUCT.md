# Code of Conduct

Short version: be kind, assume good faith, and keep disagreement about the work.

## Expected

- Critique code, numbers and sources — not people.
- Say what you tried and what you expected; a reproducible report beats a long opinion.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change to protect the kernel contract, even a well-built one.
- Keep threads in the language they started in; Chinese and English are both fine.

## Not expected

- Harassment, dismissive "just read the docs", or review comments about a person's level.
- Unrequested rewrites of files a PR does not need.
- Financial advice framed as certainty.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close or revert contributions that break the rules. Repeated or serious cases can lead to blocked
participation. Disagree with a call? Say so once in the thread, with the reasoning; the maintainer answers or revisits.

This is a small project, so the bar is simple: leave the thread more useful than you found it.
